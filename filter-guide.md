# Filter guide

What each of the nine built-in filters is tuned for, and what kind of
reference image will best showcase it as a thumbnail.

Grounded in the ops chains in `pipeline.jsx` (`PRESETS_LIST`) — channel
saturation, tone curves, bloom, halation, etc.

---

## 1. PROVIA · STANDARD — *daily driver*
- **Strengths:** balanced, slightly boosted contrast/sat — looks "right" on almost anything.
- **Best subject:** everyday / mixed scenes. Street, travel snapshots, casual portraits.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a neutral, well-lit scene with skin tones + foliage + sky — proves it doesn't push any one color.

## 2. VELVIA · VIVID — *landscape stock*
- **Strengths:** heavy green/blue/red channel saturation, +25 % contrast.
- **Best subject:** **landscape, nature, foliage, sky, autumn color.** National-park stuff.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a saturated landscape — mountains + lake, autumn forest, blue sky with white clouds. Avoid faces (oversaturates skin).

## 3. ASTIA · SOFT — *portrait stock*
- **Strengths:** soft contrast curve, warm WB, reduced green saturation (kills sickly green skin cast), boosted red saturation.
- **Best subject:** **portraits, skin tones, wedding/lifestyle.**
- **Thumbnail pick:** close- or medium-portrait with natural light. Single face, soft background.

## 4. CLASSIC CHROME · DOCUMENTARY — *travel doc*
- **Strengths:** desaturated, slightly warm, cyan-shifted shadows, lifted blacks. The "magazine photojournalism" look.
- **Best subject:** **street photography, travel, urban documentary, overcast cityscape, markets.**
- **Thumbnail pick:** a candid street/market scene with muted colors and human interest — not a vibrant landscape.

## 5. ETERNA · CINEMA — *flat profile*
- **Strengths:** very flat curve, heavy desaturation, slight cool tint — log-style cinema base.
- **Best subject:** **moody scenes, film stills, interiors, fashion editorial, low-contrast natural light.**
- **Thumbnail pick:** indoor scene with window light, or a wide moody shot with shadow detail. Not bright/colorful.

## 6. ACROS · MONOCHROME — *B&W*
- **Strengths:** monochrome conversion with heavy grain and a strong S-curve.
- **Best subject:** **anything with strong texture, architecture, portraits, street.** Tone/shape matter more than color.
- **Thumbnail pick:** high-contrast B&W-friendly scene — architectural lines, a textured face, fog/silhouette. Pick a frame where color isn't the subject.

## 7. SYNTHWAVE · NEON DUSK — *retro-future*
- **Strengths:** magenta/pink push in shadows, blue lift, bloom on highlights.
- **Best subject:** **sunsets, neon signs, dusk cityscape, palms-and-pink-sky, retro car/arcade aesthetic.**
- **Thumbnail pick:** a sunset with a strong horizon line + magenta/orange sky, or anything with bright point lights to show the bloom.

## 8. JAPAN NIGHT · TUNGSTEN — *CineStill 800T look*
- **Strengths:** orange/teal split, red halation around highlights, tungsten WB.
- **Best subject:** **night street, Tokyo/HK style alleys, neon signage, rainy reflections, car headlights.**
- **Thumbnail pick:** night scene with neon/signage and point lights. Halation only sings when there are bright sources against dark surroundings.

## 9. VINTAGE MUTE · FADED — *matte film*
- **Strengths:** matte/lifted blacks, desaturated greens & blues, warm tint — "scanned old film" feel.
- **Best subject:** **lifestyle, soft-light portraits, hazy outdoor scenes, film-emulation snapshots, beach/golden hour.**
- **Thumbnail pick:** a soft-light outdoor scene, person + environment, or hazy backlit shot. Avoid sharp high-contrast subjects.

---

## Practical suggestions for the thumbnail set

A few approaches to consider:

- **One photo per filter, matched to subject** (recommended) — strongest "this is what it's for" signal. ~9 small images.
- **One shared reference photo across all 9 thumbs** — fastest to scan side-by-side, but it'll favor whichever filter suits that subject and make others look bad.
- **Two reference photos** (e.g. one portrait + one landscape) cycled — middle ground.

If you go with one-per-filter, group them so neighbors don't all look the
same: portrait (Astia) next to landscape (Velvia), B&W (Acros) next to
color cinema (Eterna), and the two night-y ones (Synthwave, Japan Night)
not adjacent.
