# Filter guide

What each built-in filter is tuned for, and what kind of reference image will
best showcase it as a thumbnail. Grounded in the `userAdjust` + ops data in
`pipeline.jsx` (`PRESETS_LIST`). The pack is three groups: **vintage
cinematic** (the Pinterest-recipe wave — see TODOS.md), **clean cinematic**,
and three **legacy classics** kept from the old Fuji pack.

---

## Vintage cinematic

### 1. DUSTY · WARM MATTE
- **Look:** warm brown cast, creamy pulled highlights, matte fade, soft vignette.
- **Best subject:** trench-coat lifestyle, coffee shops, book-and-hands close-ups, muted interiors.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a beige/brown-wardrobe detail shot in soft window light.

### 2. WES · PASTEL STORYBOOK
- **Look:** low-ink pastel warmth, popped reds/yellows, flattened highlights, near-zero grain.
- **Best subject:** symmetrical compositions, colorful facades, deadpan portraits, uniforms.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a centered subject against a flat colored wall.

### 3. MISTY · 1999 HAZE
- **Look:** green-leaning haze (negative dehaze), heavy matte lift, desaturated, dreamy.
- **Best subject:** flowers, soft portraits, humid window light, nostalgia scenes.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a soft close portrait with foliage or white flowers.

### 4. RETRO METRO · 70S GOLD
- **Look:** golden-yellow cast, dense shadows, muted blues, vignette. NYC-in-1975 postcard.
- **Best subject:** street signs, downtown architecture, taxis, sun-struck city corners.
- **Thumbnail pick:** looking up at buildings + signage against an amber sky.

### 5. SEVENTIES · KODACHROME WASH
- **Look:** sun-washed warmth, hot highlights, heavy grain, faded blue.
- **Best subject:** denim, school buses, roadside Americana, striped knitwear, golden hour.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a backlit casual portrait or retro-styled group shot.

### 6. NINETIES · FADED WARM
- **Look:** warm fade with cool blue split-toned highlights, moderate grain.
- **Best subject:** sneakers, socks-and-rug detail shots, bedrooms, casual still lifes.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a floor-level detail shot (shoes, cassette, carpet).

### 7. DISPOSABLE · 1990 SNAPSHOT
- **Look:** drugstore-print greens, warm skin, dim exposure, soft contrast, vignette.
- **Best subject:** candid family-album poses, backyards, direct-flash energy.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a person mid-pose in a yard or against a fence, slightly awkward framing.

### 8. POLAROID · INSTANT FADE
- **Look:** instant-film fade — low contrast, yellow whites, green shadows, strong grain + vignette.
- **Best subject:** mirror selfies, party candids, close-up faces, flash indoor shots.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a flash-lit indoor candid.

### 9. GRUNGE · DARK FADE
- **Look:** underexposed, cold, contrasty, lifted blacks, heavy grain and vignette.
- **Best subject:** record stores, band tees, night interiors, moody still lifes, tattoos.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a dark cluttered interior or moody object study.

### 10. CINEMA GOLD · WARM CINEMATIC
- **Look:** rich golden midtones, deep velvet shadows, muted greens/blues, gentle vignette.
- **Best subject:** lamp-lit interiors, evening portraits, leather-and-wood scenes.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a person on a couch under warm practical lights.

### 11. GREEN ROOM · MUTED GREEN FILM
- **Look:** mossy green shadows, dim tungsten highlights, muted, grainy. Kitchen-table cinema.
- **Best subject:** kitchens, hallways, motion-blurred figures, domestic film-still scenes.
- **Thumbnail pick:** an indoor scene under a single ceiling light.

## Clean cinematic

### 12. BLOCKBUSTER · TEAL & ORANGE
- **Look:** clean modern grade — teal shadows, warm skin, punchy midtone contrast, zero grain.
- **Best subject:** portraits with sky/water behind, travel, action, urban dusk.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a portrait against blue water or sky at golden hour.

### 13. PORTRA · CLEAN FILM PORTRAIT
- **Look:** soft warm film, honest skin, pulled highlights, whisper of grain.
- **Best subject:** portraits, weddings, lifestyle, overcast daylight.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a natural-light medium portrait, neutral background.

### 14. CREAM · BRIGHT & AIRY
- **Look:** overexposed cream, soft whites, low saturation, weightless. No grain.
- **Best subject:** minimal interiors, flat lays, bright fashion, beach haze.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a white-on-white scene with one soft accent color.

## Legacy classics

### 15. CLASSIC CHROME · DOCUMENTARY
- **Look:** desaturated, slightly warm, cyan-shifted shadows, lifted blacks. Magazine photojournalism.
- **Best subject:** street photography, travel, urban documentary, markets.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a candid street/market scene with muted colors.

### 16. JAPAN NIGHT · TUNGSTEN
- **Look:** CineStill 800T orange/teal split, halation-ish bloom, tungsten WB.
- **Best subject:** night streets, neon signage, rainy reflections, headlights.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a neon-lit night scene with bright point lights.

### 17. ACROS · MONOCHROME
- **Look:** monochrome conversion, strong S-curve, heavy painterly grain.
- **Best subject:** texture, architecture, portraits, fog/silhouette.
- **Thumbnail pick:** a high-contrast B&W-friendly frame where color isn't the subject.

---

## Practical suggestions for the thumbnail set

One photo per filter, matched to subject (recommended) — strongest "this is
what it's for" signal. Group the strip so neighbors contrast: warm (DUSTY)
next to green (MISTY), clean (BLOCKBUSTER) between grainy ones, ACROS
somewhere it can't be mistaken for GRUNGE.
